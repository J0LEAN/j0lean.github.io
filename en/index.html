How it works

One rule, and it's handled.

Every macro fits in one sentence. You build it from three blocks, no coding needed.

1 · When…

What starts the macro

A time and days, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, headphones, the charger, the battery level, the screen turning on, an app opening…

2 · Do…

The actions, in order

Volume, ringer, Do Not Disturb, brightness, flashlight, music, alarm, timer, notification, reading a text out loud, opening an app or a link, tapping a text on screen…

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Why does Pépouze use the accessibility service?

Only for what you add to your macros: tapping a text on screen, pressing Back or Home, locking the screen, taking a screenshot, knowing which app opens.

Screen text is only read to find the one to tap, and nothing leaves your phone. Pépouze acts only on the rules you wrote. The service only turns on with your consent, after a dedicated explanation, and you can switch it off at any time.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The account is only used to back up your macros online. You can also back them up to a file, on Google Drive or on your phone, with no account at all.

Do my macros work with the screen off?

Yes, most of them: Pépouze listens for events in the background. To keep Android from putting it to sleep, choose “Battery: Unrestricted” on the app's page in Android settings. Actions that touch the screen need an unlocked phone.

Does Pépouze send text messages for me?

No. The “Prepare a text message” action opens your messaging app with the number and text ready: you're the one who taps Send.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: My account → Delete my account. Without the app, follow the account deletion page.
