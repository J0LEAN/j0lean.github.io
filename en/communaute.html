Share a macro

In Pépouze, open a macro and tap Share → Copy code. You can also publish it here straight from the app (Share → Publish to the community).

To publish from the website, sign in with Google. Your display name will be visible, never your email.

By publishing, you agree that this macro can be seen and copied by everyone. Never put a phone number, an address or a password in a shared macro.

Community rules

  • Share useful macros that do what their name says.
  • No personal data: phone numbers, addresses, names of people close to you, passwords.
  • No macros that send text messages, make calls or contact a website without the knowledge of the person installing them.
  • A macro reported several times is hidden until it's been reviewed.
