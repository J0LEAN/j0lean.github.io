Your data

Delete your Pépouze account

Pépouze is an Android app published by Pépouze, éditeur particulier. The account is optional: it's only used to back up your macros online. You can delete it at any time, along with its backup.

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pépouze, then My account (the profile button, at the top right of the home screen).
  2. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account and your online backup are deleted right away. If the app asks you to sign in again first, that's a Google security measure for deletions: sign out, sign back in, then try again.

Without the app

Email pepouze.apk@gmail.com from your Pépouze account's email address (your Google account's address if you use “Continue with Google”), with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete your account and its backup within 30 days at most, and confirm it by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Firebase Authentication): your email address, your first name if you gave it, and the link to your Google account.
  • Your online backup (Cloud Firestore): the copy of your macros, the date of the backup and the model of the phone that made it.

Pépouze keeps nothing after deletion. Google, which provides Firebase, then erases its technical copies on its own schedule.

What isn't affected

  • The macros on your phone: they stay in the app. To erase them, delete them or uninstall Pépouze.
  • A backup file you created (on Google Drive or on your phone): it belongs to you; delete it yourself if you wish.
  • Your Premium purchase: it's tied to your Google account and managed by Google Play, which keeps a record of it. Deleting your Pépouze account doesn't refund it or erase it.
  • Ads: Pépouze keeps no advertising data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

To learn everything about your data, read the privacy policy.
